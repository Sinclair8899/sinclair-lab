The **Business Decision Lab** turns everyday cross-department decisions into choices you can make yourself. Each module starts from a realistic operating event, lets you decide, and shows what your choice does to time, cash, profit and relationships with customers and suppliers. Then it shows what an AI agent could check or draft with the same data, and where people still have to decide and take responsibility. You don't need to know CCC, MRP or S&OP first; terms are explained on the page.

Start from one of two questions:

- **Market opportunity** — a competitor ships better specs at a lower price. Do you change the product, run a limited trial, or hold off? → [市場機會卡](/decision-lab/signal.html)
- **Operating crisis** — a key customer delays orders and asks for longer payment terms. How do you respond? → [3 分鐘當老闆](/decision-lab/case-boss.html)

## The cash conversion cycle, as one finance module

Most companies do not run out of cash because they are unprofitable. They run out because cash is tied up somewhere between the purchase order and the customer's payment: in receivables, in inventory, or in paying suppliers earlier than they need to. The **cash conversion cycle** (receivable days + inventory days − payable days) measures that gap. Every day of it is money the company must finance.

The theory is settled. What is hard is applying it: customers, suppliers, competitors, growth, capital spending and financing all pull the numbers in different directions, and every fix moves the cost to someone else. This practice is drawn from about 30 years of running operations and finance.

現金轉換週期（應收天數＋存貨天數−應付天數）衡量的是錢卡在下單到收款之間的時間，每一天都要有人出錢支應。理論早已沒有爭議，難的是應用；在這個實驗室裡，它是其中一項財務模組。

## What's inside

- **Two starting points** — 市場機會卡 (front line to R&D: five product decisions, response time and learning speed) and 3 分鐘當老闆 (a key customer delays orders and asks for longer payment terms: can you keep 13 weeks of cash above the safety line?).
- **Supply and demand** — boss cockpit (forecast, material policy and cash on one sheet), S&OP, customer commitments (CRM) and supplier commitments.
- **Operations** — dynamic MRP, critical-material sourcing and production control.
- **Finance** — AP four-document matching, AR collection, the cash conversion cycle lab (scenarios including an AI-server assembler, an advanced-process or memory fab and a cloud hyperscaler), and an AI compute provider's contracts, debt and residual values.

## What it does and does not show

- All companies, customers and figures are fictional teaching scenarios. The models are simplified, and every page states its assumptions.
- Every number is computed by rules written into the page, not decided by AI. The rules were checked for internal consistency: balance sheets balance, waterfalls add up, and moving an input moves the result in the expected direction.
- The pages separate three kinds of content. **Rule-based calculation**: every number. **Scenario demonstration**: preset results or replays, used for the AI steps in most modules. **Live AI**: six modules (00 boss cockpit, 04 S&OP, 04A CRM, 04B supplier commitments, 04C market opportunity card, 06 AI compute provider) send text to an open-source model hosted by Cloudflare, either to analyse what you type or to write up results the rules have already computed. The model does not do the calculations. Please do not paste real customer or company information.
- The pages record anonymous usage counts (which module, which scenario) without cookies and without storing IP addresses.
- The pages are in Traditional Chinese and work on phones, but open them in a browser. File previews in messaging apps do not run the interactive parts.
