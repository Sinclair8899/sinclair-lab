Most companies do not run out of cash because they are unprofitable. They run out because cash is tied up somewhere between the purchase order and the customer's payment: in receivables, in inventory, or in paying suppliers earlier than they need to. The **cash conversion cycle** (receivable days + inventory days − payable days) measures that gap. Every day of it is money the company must finance.

This lab turns that idea into decisions you can make yourself. Each module starts from a realistic operating event, lets you choose, and shows what your choice does to cash, profit and relationships. Then it shows what an AI agent would have done with the same data, and where people still have to decide and take responsibility.

## 現金循環實驗室（中文說明）

大多數公司缺錢，不是因為不賺錢，而是錢卡在下單到收款之間：卡在應收帳款、卡在存貨，或是比必要更早付給供應商。現金轉換週期（應收天數＋存貨天數−應付天數）衡量的就是這段時間，而每一天都要有人出錢支應。

這個實驗室把這個概念變成可以親手做的決定。每個模組都從一件真實常見的經營事件開始，讓你做選擇，再告訴你這個選擇對現金、獲利和上下游關係的影響，以及 AI agent 會怎麼處理同一批資料、哪些事情仍然要由人決定與承擔。

## What's inside

- **3 分鐘當老闆** — a key customer delays orders and asks for longer payment terms. Can you keep 13 weeks of cash above the safety line?
- **現金轉換週期決策實驗室** — seven scenarios, including an AI-server assembler, an advanced-process or memory fab, and a cloud hyperscaler. Diagnose the cycle with Porter's five forces, DuPont, capital structure and free cash flow, then project five years of financing with short-term loans, bonds or equity.
- **Six operating modules** — supply-and-demand intelligence and S&OP, dynamic MRP, critical-material sourcing, production control, AP four-document matching, and AR collection.

## What it does and does not show

- All companies, customers and figures are fictional teaching scenarios. The models are simplified, and every page states its assumptions.
- Every number is computed by rules written into the page, not decided by AI. The rules were checked for internal consistency: balance sheets balance, waterfalls add up, and moving an input moves the result in the expected direction.
- One module, the sales visit-note analysis in S&OP, sends your text to an open-source model hosted by Cloudflare and returns structured intelligence and action items. Please do not paste real customer or company information.
- The pages record anonymous usage counts (which module, which scenario) without cookies and without storing IP addresses.
- The pages are in Traditional Chinese and work on phones, but open them in a browser. File previews in messaging apps do not run the interactive parts.
